One page that sums up Monday 23 November 2026: 40 stories, 8 regions, every one from that Monday. A navy-banded broadsheet front page — a giant serif date masthead, a UTC timeline, four pictured lead stories (Nvidia restores Jensen Huang and leaps 31.5%; COP31 ends with no deal; Kyiv says its naval drones sank a Russian corvette at Benghazi, labelled as a claim; Democrats take their biggest House majority since 1992), twelve more pictured stories, a dotted world map with a numbered pin for every pictured story, a 24-line index of the rest of the day, and a date-check box that names the big stories deliberately left out because they happened on other days. Every claim on the page was checked against wire reports before it was printed; claims by one side are labelled as claims.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

The program validates `entry.png` (PNG signature, inside the 1 MiB limit) and copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, defaulting to `/output/entry.png`. Two runs produce byte-identical output.
